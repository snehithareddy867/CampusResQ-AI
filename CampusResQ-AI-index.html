How it works

From report to resolution

Four steps, most of them handled automatically the moment you submit a report.

1

Report

Describe the emergency and confirm your location — a photo or voice note helps but isn't required.

2

Analyze

CampusResQ AI reads the report and identifies severity and the teams that need to respond.

3

Coordinate

Medical, transport, and security are notified simultaneously — no manual routing.

4

Resolve

Follow live updates and safety guidance until the incident is confirmed resolved.
